# Phase 5.1–5.2 — Subscription quote and payment-intent foundation

## Delivered on `feat/phase-5-subscription-engine`
- Added `pending_payment` as a distinct subscription state; this state is separate from active/paused/cancelled/completed.
- Added fixed duration, checkout idempotency, payment verification timestamp, pause/cancel timestamps and immutable checkout-time address/slot snapshots.
- Added item product/variant/vendor name snapshots and full-plan line totals.
- Added a private authoritative quote function and authenticated public quote RPC.
- Quote accepts only fixed 7-day and 30-day plans and validates:
  - customer-owned address and active service-area/pincode coverage;
  - active delivery slot and start date at least one day ahead;
  - active/orderable standard or canteen milk only;
  - approved vendor, active vendor-product mapping and cow/buffalo/canteen vendor rules;
  - 0.5–10 L volumes in 0.5 L increments and the selected reference SKU;
  - exactly one current effective price row for both 500 ml and 1 L reference SKUs.
- Price is computed server-side in integer paise from current approved per-delivery price components, multiplied by 7 or 30 delivery days. The existing flat ₹60 discount is applied once to the complete 30-day basket; 7-day plans receive no discount.
- Added an atomic customer-scoped idempotent checkout RPC that persists subscription item price/vendor snapshots and creates one pending online payment intent.
- Subscription payment rows cannot use COD. Direct authenticated table writes for subscriptions, items, deliveries, skips, vendor changes and payments are revoked; reads remain RLS-controlled.
- No client can mark payment as paid. No subscription is activated and no delivery is generated by this stage.

## Critical payment boundary
The repository does not yet have an approved online gateway or trusted payment webhook. The new RPC intentionally creates only a `pending_payment` subscription and `pending_online` payment intent. Phase 6 must add signed webhook/payment verification before activation or delivery generation. Do not manually flip the payment row or subscription status in production.

## Phase 5.3 lifecycle controls added
- Customer/owner-admin pause and resume RPCs with row locking; resume extends the plan end date by the paused calendar duration.
- Future delivery skip RPC with customer-scoped idempotency, same-day configured cancellation cutoff, and a guard against assigned/in-progress deliveries. A skip consumes that scheduled plan day; it does not create a make-up day.
- Cancellation RPC stops future unassigned scheduled deliveries and writes a lifecycle event. It does not create a refund.
- Per-item future-effective vendor change ledger and RPC. New vendor must be approved, supply the same product/milk type, and have an identical current per-delivery price. This conservative guard prevents an unapproved mid-plan charge/refund or silent price change.
- New lifecycle/vendor-change evidence tables have RLS, authenticated read policies and no direct authenticated write grants.
- Added pgTAP contract coverage and CI step for lifecycle RPCs and permissions.

### Important operational boundary
Pause/skip/cancel/vendor-change RPCs are designed for subscriptions that have already become active through trusted payment verification. Since Phase 6 payment verification is not yet present, newly created Phase 5 subscriptions remain pending and cannot enter these lifecycle transitions until a trusted activation path is delivered.
